What SightAware does and does not do

SightAware by Nonnanest is a wellness awareness device. It reads skin temperature from a short distance, with no wearable, and alerts you to changes. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any illness or condition. It is not a medical device.

When to call your pediatrician

If you are concerned about your baby's health at any point, contact your pediatrician or qualified healthcare provider. Trust your parent instincts. A wellness monitor supports awareness. It does not replace medical guidance.
