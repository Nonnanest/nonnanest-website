What SightAware does and does not do

SightAware™ by Nonnanest is a wellness awareness device. It reads your baby's skin temperature from up to 4 feet away, with no wearable, and alerts you when a reading passes the level you set. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or condition.

When to call your pediatrician

If you are concerned about your baby's health at any point, contact your pediatrician or qualified healthcare provider. Trust your parent instincts. A wellness monitor supports awareness. It does not replace medical guidance.
