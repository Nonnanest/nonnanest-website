# Nonnanest website guardrails

## Names
- Company: "Nonnanest", always one word.
- Product: "SightAware™ by Nonnanest" on first mention per page, "SightAware" after. Use ™, never ®.

## Regulatory language (non-negotiable)
- SightAware is a wellness device, not a medical device.
- Always say "skin temperature". Never: body temperature, core temperature, fever, illness, sick, diagnose/diagnosis, detects, emergency, saves lives.
- "Medical device" appears only inside the disclaimer sentence.
- Say "contactless", never "contact-free". No monitoring or tracking framed as surveillance.
- Any page that describes the skin temperature feature carries this disclaimer, verbatim:
  "Nonnanest is a wellness device, not a medical device, and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or condition. Skin temperature readings are for informational purposes only to help parents monitor general wellness and comfort. For any health concerns, always consult a pediatrician or qualified healthcare provider."

## Connectivity language
- Say: direct 2.4 GHz connection between camera and parent screen; fully offline; no Wi-Fi, no app, no cloud, no subscription.
- Never mention FHSS, frequency hopping, encryption, "secure"/"encrypted", or EMF.

## Voice
- Never name competitor brands.
- No em dashes anywhere in copy.
- No superlatives or hype ("best", "#1", "most awarded", "revolutionary"). The only exception is quoting an official award title.
- "Award-winning" and "2026 award winner" are fine. They're facts.
- Third-party quotes are verbatim. Never edit them.
- Don't invent product facts. If a fact isn't in the briefs or existing site copy, ask.
