Nonnanest Contactless Baby Wellness Monitor Now Available for Pre-Order: No WiFi, No App, No Subscription

A parent-founded Charleston startup opens pre-orders for the first baby monitor that reads skin temperature from across the crib without wearables or an internet connection

CHARLESTON, SC — Nonnanest, Inc. today announced that its patent-pending contactless baby wellness monitor is now available for pre-order at nonnanest.com/shop. Priced at $329 with no recurring fees, the Nonnanest monitor is the first to combine infrared skin temperature awareness with a fully offline, FHSS-secured architecture and a dedicated parent display that works independently of any smartphone.

Why a Dad Built a Baby Monitor That Works Without WiFi

Nonnanest founder Nathan Overbey designed the monitor after a personal experience with his infant son. One night, Overbey noticed his son’s skin felt unusually warm, but the baby monitor in the nursery could only report the room temperature. There was no record of when the change began or how long it had been building.

After months of research, Overbey found that no baby monitor on the market offered contactless skin temperature awareness that worked without WiFi, without a wearable sensor, and without requiring a smartphone app. So he built one.

“The baby monitor category has been surprisingly stagnant. Most monitors still offer audio and video and not much else. The ones that have added connectivity often come with trade-offs that parents don’t realize until they’re deep into the setup: another app competing for your attention, another subscription, and security vulnerabilities over your child’s nursery that most families don’t even know exist.”

— Nathan Overbey, Founder & CEO, Nonnanest

How Contactless Skin Temperature Awareness Works

The Nonnanest nursery camera contains a proprietary infrared sensor array that reads surface skin temperature from distances up to four feet, without anything touching or attached to the baby. The technology works on the same principle as the no-touch thermometers used in pediatric offices.

When skin temperature shifts outside a parent-set comfort range, the system delivers a gentle alert to the dedicated parent unit. Parents can view real-time skin temperature alongside the live video feed at any time, giving them one more layer of passive awareness while their baby sleeps.

What Makes Nonnanest Different from WiFi Baby Monitors

Most modern baby monitors connect to a home WiFi network, store data on cloud servers, and require a smartphone app to operate. Nonnanest takes a different approach. The entire system runs on FHSS (Frequency Hopping Spread Spectrum), a secure wireless protocol that creates a direct, encrypted connection between the nursery camera and the parent unit. The signal hops across 80+ frequency channels per second, never touches the internet, and requires no accounts, no cloud storage, and no external servers.

The result is a baby monitor where all data stays physically local to your home. There is nothing to hack remotely because there is no remote connection.

“We’re not anti-technology. We’re very pro-innovation. But every design decision we made started with the question: does this serve the parent, or does it serve the product?”

— Nathan Overbey, Founder & CEO, Nonnanest

A Dedicated Parent Display Instead of a Phone App

Nonnanest includes a dedicated 5-inch HD handheld parent unit rather than relying on a smartphone app. The decision was intentional: a phone serves dozens of purposes and competes for attention with notifications, calls, and other apps. A dedicated display does one thing. It shows you your baby, their skin temperature, and the room conditions. It works at 2 AM without unlocking a phone, without worrying about battery life, and without any other app interrupting the feed.

What Comes in the Box
