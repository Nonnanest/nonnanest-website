SightAware™ by Nonnanest wins the 2026 ZestYears Golden Grandparent Award for Excellence in Baby Monitoring

The contactless skin temperature baby monitor with a dedicated parent screen, recognized after independent family testing.

SightAware™ by Nonnanest has been named a 2026 winner of the ZestYears Golden Grandparent Award for Excellence in Baby Monitoring, following independent product testing by ZestYears.

SightAware is a contactless skin temperature baby monitor with a dedicated 5-inch parent screen. No phone, no app, no home WiFi, and no monthly subscription. Caregivers set a skin temperature threshold that fits their baby, and SightAware gives a gentle audible alert if it's crossed. Nothing is worn by the baby.

ZestYears testers called out the clear video and night vision, two-way audio, contactless skin temperature readings, a local camera-to-display connection that runs offline, and no recurring fee.

What the ZestYears Golden Grandparent Award recognizes

The ZestYears Golden Grandparent Awards recognize products that earn a real place in a family's daily life. Winners are selected after independent testing by grandparents, caregivers, and family product testers. SightAware was tested on how it performs during naps and bedtime.

What testers noticed about contactless skin temperature awareness

Most baby monitors report the temperature of the room. SightAware reads skin temperature from the baby, contactless, from up to 4 feet away. The reading sits next to the room temperature on the parent screen, so caregivers can see whether it's the room or the baby.

Why skin temperature awareness matters when a baby feels warm at night

A warm baby at night can mean a lot of things. Sometimes it's the room, a snug swaddle, or warm pajamas. Sometimes it's your baby, and that's worth paying attention to. The hard part at 2am is telling the difference without waking anyone.

SightAware was built for exactly that moment. The camera reads skin temperature contactlessly from across the room and shows it next to the room temperature on the dedicated parent screen. A caregiver can see the number, and how it changes, without walking in, picking up a phone, or waking the baby. Caregivers set a skin temperature threshold that fits their baby, and SightAware gives a gentle audible alert if it's crossed.

A skin temperature reading is not a thermometer reading, and SightAware doesn't tell you why your baby is warm. If your baby feels warm and something seems off, check with a thermometer and call your pediatrician.

Why the dedicated parent screen matters

SightAware uses a dedicated 5-inch HD screen. No phone. No app. No account. The screen pairs directly to the camera and stays fully offline. Caregivers see video, hear two-way audio, and read skin temperature and room temperature on one simple display, on the nightstand at bedtime or the kitchen counter during a nap.

That also makes care easy to share. A grandparent or sitter can pick up the screen and help, with nothing to download and nothing to log into.

What SightAware does

  • Contactless skin temperature reading from up to 4 feet, with nothing worn by the baby
  • Dedicated 5-inch HD parent screen with two-way audio and lullabies
  • Infrared night vision for dark-room viewing
  • Direct camera-to-screen connection, fully offline, with no WiFi, no cloud, and no subscription

What SightAware does not do

SightAware does not diagnose anything or tell you why your baby is warm. It does not offer motion, breathing, or stillness alerts. It does not stream outside the home.

When to call your pediatrician

SightAware gives caregivers skin temperature awareness during naps and bedtime. It is not a thermometer and not a substitute for a provider's judgment. If your baby seems unwell, call your pediatrician or a qualified healthcare provider.
